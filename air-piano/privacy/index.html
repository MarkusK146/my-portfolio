What stays on your device

MIDI input and touch input generate piano sound and update the keyboard display on your iPhone. Air Piano does not record microphone audio or upload your performances. The piano recordings are bundled with the app.

The app stores piano selection, sound and metronome settings, setup progress, and headphone-model confirmations in its local storage. Audio-output identifiers remember those confirmations locally; they are excluded from exported reports. Prepared sound files are also stored locally.

Preferences and cached sound files remain until removed with the app. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading preserves it. Backups and restoration follow your iOS settings. We do not receive those local preferences through an Air Piano server.

Diagnostics you choose to share

Starting a diagnostic session captures device/iOS details, MIDI-source and audio-route names, sound settings, session dates, MIDI dispatch durations and diagnostic statistics. It captures neither your audio nor externally measured acoustic latency.

Export JSON creates a temporary report and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose whether and where to send it. Reports contain the test notes you enter; notes and device names can contain personal information. They exclude raw system-uptime counters and audio-output identifiers.

Sessions are held in memory. Exported files remain in temporary app storage until removed by the system or with the app; copies you save or send elsewhere remain at those destinations. If you send a report to us, we use it only to investigate the issue and improve reliability, as described under support below.

When you use TestFlight

Apple automatically collects beta crash logs and usage information and shares it with the developer. This may include device, installation and session information and, depending on the invitation, your name and email. Apple also shares feedback and attachments you choose to submit.

We use this information to diagnose bugs and improve Air Piano, based on our legitimate interest in running and improving the beta (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use it for advertising or pass TestFlight feedback to unrelated third parties. Apple controls collection and availability within TestFlight under TestFlight & Privacy. This is separate from the app’s optional JSON export.

When you contact support

We receive your email address, the name you provide, your message, correspondence metadata and any attachments you send. We use them to reply and investigate the problem. Providing this information is voluntary; without a way to reply or sufficient problem details, we may be unable to resolve your request. Playing the app does not require contacting support.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to requests and maintaining the app (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), or Article 6(1)(b) where needed to fulfil a contract or respond to a related pre-contract request. Applicable legal retention obligations are based on Article 6(1)(c).

We retain correspondence and reports only for as long as needed to resolve the request, investigate related issues or meet applicable legal obligations. We remove identifying details from retained bug notes when they are no longer necessary. You can request deletion using the contact above.

Messages sent to our support address are forwarded to an inbox managed by the publisher. The services handling the forwarding and the receiving inbox process message content, correspondence metadata and attachments to deliver and store the correspondence.

Before publication: The publisher has created the support forwarding address. Confirm successful delivery, the reply sender address, both email providers and their applicable privacy/transfer arrangements before completing this section.

These Air Piano pages use local styles and system fonts. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded media, cookies or browser-storage code. Opening external links or an email application takes you to services with their own privacy practices.
