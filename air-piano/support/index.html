FIRST NOTES

Connect. Choose. Play.

  1. Open Air Piano. Wait for the piano to finish loading.
  2. Choose your input. Connect a class-compliant USB-MIDI keyboard, or select Keyboard on Play to use the screen.
  3. Check your output. Select speakers or headphones using the iOS audio-output picker.
  4. Make it yours. Tap the piano artwork for Grand Piano or Warm Ballad, then adjust effects and volume.

A keyboard and AirPods are optional. The current app is for iPhone with iOS 18.6 or later.

A few quick answers.

I can’t hear the piano

Check the iPhone media volume, the app’s volume and the active audio output. Wait for sound preparation. If shown, tap Resume Audio. In Settings → Piano Sound, Test Note checks sound without a keyboard.

My USB keyboard isn’t recognized

Use a data-capable USB cable. Some controllers need a powered adapter or hub. In Settings → Audio & Devices, set Input source to Automatic, then reconnect the keyboard. A network MIDI session is different from a physical USB keyboard.

How do I use the on-screen keys?

On Play, choose Keyboard in the Visualizer / Keyboard switcher. Tap the keys and change octave with the octave controls. Visualizer displays incoming MIDI notes; it is not the playable keyboard.

AirPods are connected, but I hear delay

Check that iOS is actually sending sound to your AirPods. Bluetooth response varies. A connected card or confirmed headphone model does not prove that a low-latency system mode is active. Air Piano cannot force that mode.

What happens in the sound check?

The first melody round deliberately adds 80 ms of software delay. Holding Hold to feel the difference for two seconds removes that added delay for the second round. Ordinary Play has no added demo delay. Replay it in Settings → Setup & sound check.

How do I adjust effects or stop a stuck note?

Turn on Reverb or Echo, then hold its dial and slide up or down. In Settings → Piano Sound, Stop All Notes clears held notes. No Pedal Mode adds a natural release tail when you have no sustain pedal.

Help us reproduce it.

Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, keyboard/headphone model and the steps that caused the issue. TestFlight testers can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.
